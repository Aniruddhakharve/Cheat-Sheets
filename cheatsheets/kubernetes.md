# Kubernetes (K8s) Cheat Sheet

## Quick Commands
- `kubectl get pods -A`
- `kubectl describe pod <name>`
- `kubectl logs <pod> -f`
- `kubectl apply -f manifest.yaml`
- `kubectl rollout status deployment/<name>`

## Interview Pointers
- Pod, Deployment, Service
- ConfigMap vs Secret
- Liveness vs Readiness probes
- HPA basics
